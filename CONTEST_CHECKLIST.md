## Judging areas — evidence, not predicted scores
| Area | Implemented evidence |
|---|---|
| Fun and playability | Fair awareness, visible interactions, quick retries, optional secret and replay challenges |
| Creativity | Affectionate festival stealth plus Mushak’s small passage and culturally linked puzzles |
| Completeness | One full level, all objectives, celebration, result, replay, local records |
| Ease of use and look | Progressive prompts, clean HUD, distinct rooms, clear puzzle patterns, responsive controls |
| Technical quality | Dependency-free static build, exact occlusion, navigation tests, safe audio/storage fallback |
| Demo and explanation | Short recording script, concise submission note, understandable code answers |

The festival-element examples in the rules are inspiration, not a requirement to include every one. Visarjan and eco-murti activities are not added. Organizers may provide shared leaderboard/dashboard arrangements; this package intentionally retains local-only records and makes no claim of integration.
